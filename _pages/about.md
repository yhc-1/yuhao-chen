**My research turns the real world into interactive digital twins — virtual counterparts that can be explored, manipulated, and simulated.** I work across scales, from handheld objects to rooms and rinks to whole construction sites, spanning 2D and 3D perception, video understanding, 4D reconstruction, and generative modeling.

## Background
I am a Research Assistant Professor and a PI at the [Vision and Image Processing Lab](https://vip.uwaterloo.ca/), Department of Systems Design Engineering, University of Waterloo. I hold NSERC Discovery Grant as Principal Investigator ("Digital Twinning of Food for Interactive Analysis and Simulation"), is Co-PI on the NRC-funded iVISION project, and is a co-investigator on a Miovision / Collaborate 2 Commercialize traffic analytics project. I joined VIP in 2020 as a postdoctoral fellow with [Prof. Alexander Wong](https://uwaterloo.ca/systems-design-engineering/profile/a28wong), became a Research Associate in 2022, and a Research Assistant Professor in 2023. I hold a B.Sc. (2015) and Ph.D. (2019) in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Purdue University, where I worked with [Prof. Edward J. Delp](https://engineering.purdue.edu/~ace/) in the [VIPER lab](https://engineering.purdue.edu/~ips/).

Co-supervising 5 PhD and 9 MASc students &middot; 130 undergraduates since 2022 &middot; 93 papers ([Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qc6CJjYAAAAJ))

## Research
- **Food** — dietary monitoring from video, 3D geometry and portion measurement, generative food models
- **Sports** — 3D mesh recovery, rink registration, action recognition, and tracking from broadcast video
- **Construction** — long-term UAV site monitoring, change detection, level-of-detail Gaussian splatting
- **Robotics** — grasping and bin picking, Gaussian-splatting SLAM, 3D scene graphs, embodied question answering
